These Referral Terms apply to Nubie's "Share Nubie" referral program, including referral links, discount codes, gift codes, and milestone rewards.

1. Eligibility

The Share Nubie referral program is available to all registered Nubie account holders in good standing. You must have a valid Nubie account to generate a referral link and earn rewards.

2. How It Works

Each member receives a unique referral link containing their personal referral code. When a friend clicks this link, registers for a Nubie account, and completes their first purchase, the referral is considered "completed." Both the referrer and the referred friend become eligible for rewards.

3. Reward Structure

Rewards are issued based on the number of completed referrals (friends who made a purchase):

  • Milestone 1 (1st friend purchases): Both the referrer and friend receive a 15% discount code valid for their next order.
  • Milestone 2 (2nd friend purchases): The referrer receives a gift code to claim a Mini Gift Set with their next order.
  • Milestone 3 (3rd friend purchases): The referrer receives a gift code to claim a Discovery Set with their next order.

Each referred friend always receives the same 15% discount code upon their first purchase, regardless of which milestone the referrer has reached.

4. Code Types & Usage

Discount Codes (SHARE15-XXXXXX): Apply at checkout in the promo code field for 15% off your order subtotal. One-time use. Expires 30 days from issue.

Gift Codes (GIFT-MINI-XXXXXX / GIFT-DISC-XXXXXX): Apply at checkout in the gift card field to claim the physical reward (Mini Gift Set or Discovery Set) with your order. One-time use. Expires 60 days from issue.

Codes cannot be combined with other promotional offers unless explicitly stated. Codes have no cash value and cannot be resold or transferred.

5. Reward Cap

Members may invite an unlimited number of friends. However, milestone rewards are capped at the first 3 friends who complete a purchase. Referrals beyond the 3rd completed purchase will not generate additional rewards for the referrer.

6. Physical Gift Fulfillment

Mini Gift Sets and Discovery Sets are physical items. When you apply a gift code during checkout, the item will be included with your order shipment. Gift availability is subject to stock. Nubie reserves the right to substitute gifts of equal or greater value if stock is unavailable.

7. Abuse Prevention

The following are prohibited and may result in referral removal, code invalidation, or account suspension:

  • Self-referral (referring your own account or accounts you control)
  • Creating fake or duplicate accounts to earn referral rewards
  • Sharing referral codes on unauthorized coupon or deal-sharing websites
  • Any fraudulent activity designed to exploit the referral program

Each user can only be referred once. The first referral code used during registration is permanent and cannot be changed.

8. Modifications

Nubie reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the Share Nubie referral program at any time with reasonable notice. Any earned but unused rewards will remain valid until their expiration date.
