What is Mark?

Mark is Nubie’s reward currency. You can earn Marks through purchases and meaningful actions, then use them to redeem rewards, unlock tier benefits, and move deeper into the Nubie Circle.

Mark is not just a point. It is a symbol of every step you take with Nubie.

Mark Rules

To keep the Circle fair for every member, Mark Rewards are subject to simple rules.

  • Marks are available only for registered Nubie accounts.
  • Marks cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Marks cannot be transferred to another account.
  • Marks may be deducted if an order is canceled, returned, or refunded.
  • Marks from purchases are calculated from the paid product value, excluding shipping fees and discounts.
  • Some rewards may have minimum order requirements.
  • Some rewards are available while stocks last.
  • Nubie may adjust Mark rules, earning rates, or tier benefits with reasonable notice.
  • Abuse, fraud, fake accounts, or suspicious activity may result in account suspension or Mark removal.
